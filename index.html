More to explore

Six favourites for every kind of study break. Open Details to learn more or Read more for a closer look.

Honey Oat Latte

A smooth espresso with creamy oat milk and a little honey.

Details
A mellow pick-me-up for a busy morning. The honey adds a gentle sweetness without hiding the coffee flavour.

Honey Oat Latte

If you like a warm, lightly sweet coffee, this is a great place to start. Oat milk gives it a soft, creamy finish.

Iced Vanilla Coffee

Cold coffee, milk and vanilla for an easy afternoon break.

Details
This one is chilled and refreshing. A touch of vanilla makes the coffee taste a little sweeter and softer.

Iced Vanilla Coffee

Try this when you want coffee without the heat. The ice keeps it crisp, while vanilla rounds out the flavour.

Matcha Morning

Earthy green tea whisked until smooth and mixed with milk.

Details
Matcha has a slightly grassy taste and a lovely green colour. It is a nice change from an espresso-based drink.

Matcha Morning

This is for anyone who enjoys tea more than coffee. The creamy milk makes the matcha gentler and easy to sip.

Cinnamon Chai

A cosy blend of black tea, milk and warming spices.

Details
Cinnamon and cardamom give chai its warm flavour. It is especially good on a chilly day between classes.

Cinnamon Chai

Choose this if you like fragrant, spiced drinks. It is comforting, aromatic and made for slow afternoons.

Classic Cappuccino

Rich espresso topped with a thick layer of airy milk foam.

Details
A cappuccino has a stronger coffee flavour than a latte, balanced with warm milk and a generous cap of foam.

Classic Cappuccino

A simple café classic. It has enough espresso character to wake you up, with foam to keep each sip light.

Hot Chocolate

A chocolatey cup topped with milk foam and marshmallows.

Details
For the days when coffee is not the mood, this drink is all about a smooth, rich chocolate flavour.

Hot Chocolate

This is a sweet, caffeine-light alternative to coffee. Perfect for a quiet study break or a chat with friends.
